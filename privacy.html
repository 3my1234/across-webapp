ATL Xpress

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

ATL Xpress (Atlantic Express) is operated by Atlantic Shansu Logistics Limited. This policy covers our customer marketplace, provider workspace and related support services. Contact support@atlxpres.com with privacy questions.

Information we use

  • Account and profile details you provide, such as your name, email, phone number, address and chosen profile image.
  • Provider business information, listings, product and service images, verification documents and availability that you submit.
  • Orders, bookings, subscription status, payment references, refunds, rewards and transaction history needed to operate the marketplace.
  • Messages, attachments, Town Hall requests, reviews, reports and Support conversations you create.
  • Location when you choose location-based features, device push-notification identifiers, notification preferences and technical request/security information.
  • Listing-discovery context and promotional interactions, such as listing impressions and contact taps. These support recommendations, rotation and aggregate provider performance reporting.

Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate accounts, show customer and provider workspaces, verify providers, display eligible listings, connect customers with businesses, process transactions, deliver messages and notifications, handle disputes and deletion requests, maintain security and improve reliability. Recommendations use listing context, location filters and recent topics; private chat content is not a recommendation input.

Visibility and service providers

Published business profiles, listings, Town Hall content and reviews are visible to their intended marketplace or community audience. Direct messages and Support conversations are available to their participants and authorized staff where required for support, reports or safety. Verification documents are not public listing images.

Authentication providers, including Privy and Google when selected, help sign you in. Flutterwave processes supported marketplace and subscription payments. Hosting, storage, email, push-notification and map providers process information needed for those functions. Map requests may reveal your IP address and the map area you view. We may disclose information when necessary to comply with legal obligations or investigate fraud and abuse.

Your choices and permissions

You can edit supported profile details, manage notification settings, control available provider promotion preferences and request account deletion. Camera, microphone, photo/file and location access are used for features you choose; you can manage permissions in your phone settings. Some features will be unavailable without the relevant permission.

Retention and account deletion

We retain information as needed to provide the service, resolve transactions and disputes, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data using Settings in the app or our account deletion page. A customer and linked provider business use one account. We will explain any records that must be retained and the reason when handling your request. Removing the app or clearing local chat history does not delete your server account.

Security and updates

We use access controls and other safeguards to protect information. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this policy as the service changes; the date above identifies the current version.
