Atlantic Shansu Logistics Limited

Factory-direct logistics, procurement, and e-commerce.

ATLANTIC SHANSU LOGISTICS LIMITED is an end-to-end procurement and supply chain logistics platform. We enable Nigerian businesses and consumers to seamlessly source, clear, and transport commercial goods and electronics directly from international manufacturers to Nigeria. We use Flutterwave to safely collect payments for logistics services, handling fees, and freight aggregation.
