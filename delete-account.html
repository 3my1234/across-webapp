ATL Xpress

Request account and personal data deletion

This page is for ATL Xpress (Atlantic Express), operated by Atlantic Shansu Logistics Limited. You can make a request even if the app is no longer installed.

From the app

  1. Sign in and open Account → Settings.
  2. Choose “Request account deletion” and review the explanation.
  3. Type DELETE, submit the request and follow its progress in Support.

Without the app

Email support@atlxpres.com with the subject “ATL Xpress account and data deletion request”. Use your registered email address where possible. If you cannot access it, explain that so we can help verify account ownership. Never send your password, sign-in codes, card number or private verification documents in an initial email.

What your request covers

Request deletion of your account and associated personal data, including the personal profile and linked provider business where applicable. You may also identify particular data you want removed. Support will confirm ownership, explain the processing steps and reply with the outcome. Submitting a request does not immediately delete the account or settle outstanding transactions.

Orders, payments, disputes, fraud-prevention or other legally required records may need to be retained. We will explain any retention that applies when handling your request. Published content, business records and shared conversations will be reviewed as part of that process. Clearing chat history on your phone or uninstalling the app is not an account-deletion request.
